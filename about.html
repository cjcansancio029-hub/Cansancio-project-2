About KCC

A tradition of excellence and service

Kabankalan Catholic College is committed to providing holistic education that develops competent professionals, responsible citizens, and persons of faith. Our learning community combines academic rigor with values formation and community engagement.

We prepare students not only for successful careers, but also for lives marked by integrity, compassion, and service to society.

Our Vision

A dynamic Catholic educational community forming globally competent and socially responsible leaders.

Our Mission

To provide excellent instruction, Christian formation, research, and responsive community service.

Our Values

Faith, scholarship, integrity, excellence, stewardship, and service guide every member of KCC.
