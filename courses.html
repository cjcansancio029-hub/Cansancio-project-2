Basic Education

Kindergarten to Grade 6

Elementary Department

The Elementary Department welcomes learners from Kindergarten through Grade 6 into a caring, Christ-centered environment. Here, strong academics go hand in hand with Catholic values, helping students grow both in the classroom and in their faith.

Our teachers know each child by name. With small class sizes, updated facilities, and a close-knit school community, every student gets the attention and encouragement they need.

Why Choose KCC Elementary?

  • Catholic values integrated in all subjects
  • DepEd-aligned K-12 curriculum
  • Small class sizes for personalized learning
  • Dedicated and experienced faculty
  • Safe and nurturing campus environment
  • Regular spiritual formation activities
  • Co-curricular and extracurricular programs
  • Parent involvement and engagement

Strong Academic Foundation

Arts & Creativity

Grades 7 to 10

Junior High School

The Junior High School Department covers Grades 7 through 10, building on the elementary years and preparing students for Senior High. We follow the DepEd K-12 curriculum while keeping Catholic faith formation at the center.

Students take part in academic competitions, sports, cultural events, and community outreach — gaining experiences that shape them into confident, well-rounded young people.

Learning Areas (Grades 7-10)

  • English
  • Mathematics
  • Science
  • Filipino
  • Araling Panlipunan
  • MAPEH (Music, Arts, PE, Health)
  • Edukasyon sa Pagpapakatao (EsP)
  • Technology and Livelihood Education (TLE)
  • Christian Living / Religious Education
